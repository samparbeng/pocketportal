Pocket Portal Privacy Policy

Effective 24 October 2026 (updated to cover optional sound streaming). Applies to the Pocket Portal app for Android phones and the Pocket Portal app for VR headsets.

The short version

Pocket Portal shows your phone's screen, and optionally plays its sound, in your own VR headset and lets you control the phone from there. Everything travels directly between your phone and your headset on your local Wi-Fi network. We have no servers. Pocket Portal doesn't collect, store, sell or share your personal data, and it contains no ads, analytics or tracking.

Accessibility Service

Pocket Portal includes an optional Accessibility Service called "Pocket Portal remote touch". It's off by default, and the app asks for your consent before you switch it on. When it's on, it only:

  • performs the gestures and navigation actions (Back, Home, Recents, notifications) you make in the headset viewer while sharing, and
  • reads and edits the text box you're typing into, so it can add the text you type in the headset.

It doesn't collect, log or transmit what's on your screen or what you type. You can switch it off at any time in your phone's Accessibility settings, and Pocket Portal still works as a view-only mirror without it.

Screen capture

Screen sharing uses Android's screen-capture feature, which you approve each time you start it. Android shows a status-bar indicator while sharing is on, and you can stop it at any time from the app, its notification or the status bar. Pocket Portal doesn't record or save your screen. Apps that protect their content (for example banking and streaming apps) appear black.

Permissions

  • Local network / nearby devices: to accept connections from your headset on your Wi-Fi.
  • Audio recording ("Microphone" in Android settings): Android requires this permission to capture the sound other apps are playing. Pocket Portal never turns on or records the microphone. Switch off "Stream sound" in the app to stop all sound capture.
  • Internet and Wi-Fi state: to run the local connection and show your phone's Wi-Fi address. Pocket Portal doesn't connect to any server on the internet.
  • Notifications and foreground service: to show the ongoing "sharing" notification with a Stop button.

Security

A headset can only view or control your phone after entering the 6-digit PIN shown on the phone, and only while you're sharing. Use Pocket Portal on networks you trust, such as your home Wi-Fi. You can generate a new PIN in the app at any time.

Children

Pocket Portal isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect any information from them.

Data deletion

Pocket Portal holds no data about you on any server, so there's nothing for us to delete. To remove the PIN and settings stored on your devices, uninstall the app or clear its storage in Android settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date.
