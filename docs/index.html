Pocket Portal

Your phone, inside your VR headset.

  • See your Android phone's screen as a window in your headset
  • Tap, swipe, scroll and type on it without taking the headset off
  • Everything stays on your own Wi-Fi: no accounts, servers, ads or tracking

Install Pocket Portal on your phone, tap Start sharing, then open the Pocket Portal headset app or your headset's browser and pick your phone.
